Flow 2 / 6

Flow 5 / 6

Flow 6 / 6

Doctors write cases into the rule engine

The authoring tool (desktop). A clinician builds checkpoints, dose bands, harm lists and rules, attaches a citation to every lesson, and previews as a player. A second clinician must sign it off before any trainee sees it. The same case format can be imported through the API.
